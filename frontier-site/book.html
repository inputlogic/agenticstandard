Honest Fit

Who we're right for.

A firm that never says no takes work it shouldn't. Here's what fits and what doesn't.

Strong fit

If you're here for this, we should talk.

  • You have an executive sponsor who can approve $48k
  • You've tried AI, it didn't stick, and you know why that matters
  • Your problem is capacity: "we can't take on more without more people"
  • You can decide in 30 to 90 days, not 6+ months
  • Your team lives in Slack or Teams
  • Your tools are modern enough for agents to read and write to them
Not our fit

If you're here for these, we'll point you elsewhere.

  • You want a strategy deck without deployment
  • You want someone to just "tell us what AI to buy"
  • You're mid-dispute with another vendor over a failed project
  • The decision needs 6+ people who aren't in the room yet
  • Your budget is under $40k with no flexibility
  • You're heavily regulated (HIPAA, PCI) and need deep compliance work first

Somewhere in the middle? Book the call. Talking will settle it faster than reading.
