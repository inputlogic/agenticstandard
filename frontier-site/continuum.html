The Ongoing Partnership

The Agentic Continuum.

The ongoing partnership after the Foundation. We keep building, month after month.

What it is

A partnership that works the plan.

The Foundation leaves you with working agents and the Map. The Continuum is us working that plan with you. New agents ship on schedule, your team gets better every quarter, and your board gets rules it can stand behind.

Think of it as an operating partner you don't have to hire, on a contract that doesn't lock you in.

It compounds. Month three beats month one. By month twelve you're a different company.

What's included

Seven things that happen every month.

Seven things, done every month, with results you can point to. No hour banks, no menus.

01
The Build

New agents, on schedule.

New agents ship on the schedule your Map calls for, usually one or two a quarter. Each one gets the same care as your first: we watch the work, build, tune, and stabilize.

The Map stays alive too. New ideas get added, priorities shift, and the plan gets sharper every month.

02
The Fleet

We run the fleet.

Every agent we've ever built for you is watched, tuned, and hosted on Harbour, with logs and alerts built in.

When something breaks, we fix it before you file a ticket. The fleet is our job, not yours.

03
The Counsel

A partner on call.

A named senior person who knows your company. Regular working sessions, plus a direct Slack line for decisions that can't wait.

Your sponsor gets someone to think out loud with who has seen this before.

04
The People

Your team, coached.

Training continues, role by role: prompt libraries, train-the-trainer sessions, and coaching for the people who own AI in each department.

The goal is a team that doesn't need us, because they learned how to do it themselves.

05
The Fences

Rules that scale.

We keep building the guardrails: policies, audit trails, scoped access, and incident playbooks your board, lawyers, and auditors can stand behind.

This is where we lean in hardest. Clear limits are the only way autonomy scales.

06
The Reporting

Board-ready reporting.

Every quarter: your scorecard, updated and explained, with a one-page summary your sponsor can drop straight into a board agenda.

It's the report that proves the retainer pays for itself, in language your board already speaks.

07
The Plan

A plan that learns.

Every quarter the Map is refreshed. Shipped work becomes track record, new ideas come in, and priorities shift with reality.

If you ever leave, you leave with a plan sharpened by a year of work.
