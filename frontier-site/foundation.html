Frequently Asked

Common questions, answered honestly.

Six weeks, $48k, and a money-back guarantee raises questions. Here are the common ones.

What's the difference between AI and agents?

AI is the model: Claude, GPT, the engine. Most companies "have AI" as a chat window that waits for someone to type. An agent is that AI set up to act on its own: running on a schedule, watching your systems, posting in Slack, finishing tasks while your team sleeps. We deploy agents.

What if we're not ready for agents?

If you're asking, you're readier than you think. Getting ready is what the six weeks builds. By week six it isn't a question anymore: agents are running and the Map shows what to work on next.

How do you choose which agents to deploy?

We don't pick from a catalog. In weeks one and two we watch three to five of your workflows, find the ones worth automating, and agree on them with your sponsor before writing any code. The agents are built for your work, from scratch.

What happens if our IT department blocks this?

We meet IT and security in week one. The agents run on your infrastructure or ours, your choice, inside your existing rules, and go through any review your team needs. If deployment turns out to be impossible by week four, we pause and refund.

Why six weeks, specifically?

Long enough to watch the work and ship agents that hold up. Short enough that there's a date on the calendar and a result to show. Six-month projects stall; six-week sprints finish.

What's the guarantee actually cover?

One thing: is your sponsor happy at the week-six session? If not, you don't pay, or you get refunded if you already did. No "good faith effort" clauses. Happy sponsor, or refund.

What happens after week six?

Three paths. (1) Continue with The Agentic Continuum, from $20k/month, first month included if you decide within 30 days. (2) Take it in-house and let us host for $2k/month, with fixes at $200/hour. (3) Run everything yourself. Every path leaves you ahead of where you started.
