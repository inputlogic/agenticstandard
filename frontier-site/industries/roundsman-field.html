The fence around it · why this is safe to own
  • It reads a mirror of the historian. The control network does not know it exists.
  • It cannot open a valve, change a setpoint, start or stop anything. The permission does not exist, so it cannot be misused.
  • The alarm panel keeps its job. The Roundsman works the space between alarms, where trends live and shutdowns are still cheap.
  • Every find ships with its evidence. No black boxes on a regulated asset.
  • Spend is capped in the request path. Every read and every pin is on the record.
