The Range

Three tempos an agent can run.

Not every agent is an alarm. The loop is the same six steps every time; what changes is when it fires and what lands in front of a person. These are the three tempos we deploy most, and you can drive two of them right now.

Tempo 01
lives in the minutes

The Watcher

Runs all day on a short clock, says nothing for hours, and speaks the moment one load goes sideways. You answer with a judgment call. The Exception Scout on our Logistics desk is one.

Work a shift with it →

Tempo 02
lives in the night

The Reporter

Runs once, before you arrive, and hands you a finished artifact with its homework attached. No alert to answer. Your day simply starts at the decision instead of the reading. You just drove it.

Tempo 03
lives in the backlog

The Queue-worker

Runs on every item that lands: sorts it, drafts the next step, and keeps the pile at zero. You sign the plan. The Maintenance Triager on this page is one, and its demo is next in line.

Every tempo above is an example build, not a case study. Which one earns a seat on your floor first is exactly what the Readiness Map answers.
